Toneline EQ - privacy
================

Short version: Toneline EQ collects nothing and sends nothing about you anywhere.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

What stays on your PC
- Presets and settings: %APPDATA%\TonelineEQ
- Error log (only if something goes wrong): %APPDATA%\TonelineEQ\error.log.
  It is never sent anywhere. Read it before you paste it into a bug report.
- "Test my mic" records 5 seconds into %TEMP%\TonelineEQ-mic-test.wav on your PC
  and plays it back to you. It is never uploaded.
- The visualizer listens to your audio only to draw the bars, in memory.
- "Now Playing" asks Windows which song is playing, to show it in the app.
- Share codes contain only the preset's name and EQ settings, and only go
  where you paste them.

When Toneline EQ uses the internet
- Only when you search in "Find my headphones": Toneline EQ downloads AutoEQ files
  from raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub). GitHub sees a normal web request
  from your IP address, as with any website (see GitHub's privacy statement).
- "Get Equalizer APO" and the license link open your web browser.
Nothing else connects to the internet.

Removing your data
Uninstall Toneline EQ from Windows Settings > Apps, or delete %APPDATA%\TonelineEQ.

Questions: open an issue on Toneline EQ's GitHub page.
